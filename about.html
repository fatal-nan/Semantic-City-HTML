The words we use each day are drifting beyond their first definitions. Through online exchanges, shared emotions, and algorithmic generation, they are repeated, borrowed, misread—and made meaningful anew.

Here, fragments of meaning take on living forms. Beginning as drifting embryos, they glow, divide, or turn translucent through human dialogue, settling into a garden of coexistence and continual growth.

You are not merely a witness to meaning. Every sentence you offer may shape what it becomes.

Does a word end at its definition,
or begin again with its next use?

我们每天使用的词语，正在离开它们最初的解释。在网络交流、集体情绪与算法生成中，它们被重复、借用、误读，也不断获得新的意义。

在这里，意义的碎片被想象为新的生命：从漂浮的幼体出发，在对话中发光、分裂或逐渐透明，汇入一座彼此共存、持续生长的花园。

你不是意义的旁观者。你输入的每一句话，都可能改变它的下一种形态。

一个词的边界，止于解释，
还是始于下一次使用？
